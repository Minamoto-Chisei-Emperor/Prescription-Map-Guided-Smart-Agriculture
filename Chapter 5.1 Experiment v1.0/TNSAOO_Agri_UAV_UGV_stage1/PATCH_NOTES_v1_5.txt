Added ablation/baseline experiment framework:
1. runAblationBatch.m
2. getExperimentVariants.m
3. applyExperimentVariant.m
4. runIndependentPlanningBaseline.m
5. makeAblationRecord.m
6. recordsTableAppend.m
7. summarizeAblationResults.m
8. plotAblationStatistics.m

Default comparison methods:
- FullTNSAOO: original-AOO-adapted update + TIS.
- NoTIS: original-AOO-adapted update without TIS.
- SimpleAOO: stage-1 simplified AOO-inspired update + TIS.
- Independent: deterministic natural order baseline with nearest active refill and no upper-layer optimization.

Notes:
- The Independent baseline is intentionally simple. It is not a full planner; it is a no-upper-layer-optimization reference.
- Start with cfg.ablation.numFields=5 and cfg.ablation.seeds=[2026].
- Increase fields/seeds after the pipeline is verified.

Chapter 5.1 Experiment v1.0

Purpose:
Generate the second and third panels for the dataset/scenario illustration in Section 5.1.

Panel 1:
- original satellite image;
- not generated by this package.

Panel 2:
- only the real field boundary in local planar coordinates;
- no prescription patches;
- no refill stations;
- no obstacles;
- no crop rows;
- no depot.

Panel 3:
- real field boundary;
- irregular localized spraying prescription patches;
- irregular localized fertilization prescription patches;
- candidate refill stations;
- obstacle regions;
- crop-row direction;
- depot/base.

Main script:
runChapter51Experiment.m

Main output files:
results/chapter5_1_yyyymmdd_HHMMSS/panel2_field_boundary_only.png
results/chapter5_1_yyyymmdd_HHMMSS/panel3_synthetic_prescription_scenario.png

Important:
The localized prescription regions are synthetic and are generated inside real field boundaries.
They should be described in the paper as synthetic prescription patches, not as real pest or nutrient maps.

Patch v1.4.1

Fixed:
1. runBatchFields.m no longer fails with:
   "Subscripted assignment between dissimilar structures"
   / "在不同结构体之间进行下标赋值。"

Cause:
- Successful and failed batch records could have slightly different struct layouts
  or MATLAB could treat an intermediate failed assignment as a dissimilar structure.

Solution:
- Added a fixed batch record template.
- Added appendBatchRecord.m and standardizeBatchRecord.m.
- Rewrote makeBatchRecord.m and makeFailedRecord.m to fill the same template.
- runBatchFields.m now appends records through appendBatchRecord().
- Exception reports are saved as error_run_*.txt for debugging.

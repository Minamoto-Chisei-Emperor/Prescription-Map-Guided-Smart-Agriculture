Chapter 5.2 Representative Cases v1.1

Critical correction:
The previous version could generate spraying/fertilization prescription patches overlapping obstacle regions, and straight transfer paths could cross obstacles. This was logically inconsistent because obstacle regions should not contain crops and UGV/UAV ground-projection transfer paths should not enter obstacle regions.

Fixes:
1. Obstacles are generated first.
2. The operable/croppable region is computed as:
   field boundary minus obstacle regions.
3. Depot, refill stations, prescription patches, crop rows, and demand maps are generated only inside this operable region.
4. UAV and UGV transfer segments use a lightweight obstacle-aware visibility planner.
5. Coverage paths are generated only inside prescription patches that have already been clipped to the obstacle-free operable region.

New helper files:
- buildOperablePolygon.m
- isPointInFreeSpace.m
- isSegmentInFreeSpace.m
- planTransferPath.m

Remaining simplification:
The transfer planner is still a lightweight visibility-graph approximation. It fixes the obvious obstacle-crossing problem, but it is not yet the full adaptive Theta*/Hybrid A* lower-layer planner described as a later high-fidelity module.

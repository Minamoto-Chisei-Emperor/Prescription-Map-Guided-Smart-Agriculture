Chapter 5.2 Representative Cases v1.3_fast

Purpose:
Restore the fast lower-layer transfer planner for practical Chapter 5.2 figure generation, while preserving the important geometry corrections from v1.2.

Kept from v1.2:
- Obstacles are generated before prescription patches.
- The operable region is field boundary minus obstacles.
- Prescription patches, crop rows, refill stations, and depot are generated only in the obstacle-free operable region.
- Spraying patches do not overlap other spraying patches.
- Fertilization patches do not overlap other fertilization patches.
- Spraying and fertilization patches are still allowed to overlap.
- Geometry validation fields remain in representative_case_results.csv.

Changed from v1.2:
- Removed slow grid-based Theta*/row-A* transfer from the active path planner.
- Restored fast lightweight obstacle-aware visibility transfers via planTransferPath.
- This version should run much faster than v1.2.

Important:
This is the recommended version for Chapter 5.2 figure generation if v1.2 is too slow.

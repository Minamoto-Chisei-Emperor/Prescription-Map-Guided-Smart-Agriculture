Chapter 5.2 Representative Cases v1.2

Critical corrections and upgrades:

1. Same-type prescription patch non-overlap
   - Spraying patches are prevented from overlapping other spraying patches.
   - Fertilization patches are prevented from overlapping other fertilization patches.
   - Spraying and fertilization patches are allowed to overlap, because the
     same local area may require both pesticide application and fertilization.

2. Obstacle exclusion
   - Obstacle regions are generated first.
   - The operable region is computed as real field boundary minus obstacles.
   - Prescription patches, crop rows, refill stations, and depot are generated
     only inside the obstacle-free operable region.

3. Lower-layer planning upgrade
   - UAV transfers use a grid-based any-angle Theta*-style path planner.
   - UGV transfers use a row-constrained grid A* path planner.
   - Both planners avoid obstacle regions and field exterior.
   - Coverage strips are generated inside obstacle-free prescription patches.

4. Geometry validation
   - representative_case_results.csv now reports:
     geometryValid
     sprayObstacleOverlap
     fertObstacleOverlap
     spraySameTypeOverlap
     fertSameTypeOverlap
     refillObstacleViolations
     depotObstacleViolation

Important honesty note:
This version is a much stronger lower-layer implementation than the earlier
straight-line transfer prototype. However, it is still a MATLAB simulation
implementation of grid-based Theta*/row-constrained A*, not a full continuous
vehicle-dynamics Hybrid A* with Reeds-Shepp curves.
